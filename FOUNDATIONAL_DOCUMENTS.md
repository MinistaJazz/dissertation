# foundational documents

these are the texts this dissertation rests on. not in a survey way. in a *if you want to understand what i am doing, read these first* way. each one is named because the work would not exist without it.

i am not republishing them here. each text belongs to its author or its collective. i am linking out where stable sources exist and citing in full where they do not. follow the link to read in the source's own voice. that is the methodology.

---

## the combahee river collective statement (1977)

**combahee river collective.** "the combahee river collective statement." 1977. originally published in *capitalist patriarchy and the case for socialist feminism*, edited by zillah eisenstein, monthly review press, 1978.

read at: [blackpast.org/african-american-history/combahee-river-collective-statement-1977/](https://www.blackpast.org/african-american-history/combahee-river-collective-statement-1977/)

this is the document the sisterhood voice data trust descends from. the statement is the architectural blueprint of *integrated analysis and practice based upon the fact that the major systems of oppression are interlocking.* the trust is one operational expression of that politics in 2026, under different technology, under the same charge. the statement's closing line — *if Black women were free, it would mean that everyone else would have to be free since our freedom would necessitate the destruction of all the systems of oppression* — is the line my work tries to make infrastructure of.

i name the collective in the dissertation's lane V, methods chapter, and acknowledgements. the sisterhood in *sisterhood voice data trust* is named for the 1977 gathering of Black women writers who called themselves the sisterhood, in lineage with combahee. the politics is older than the trust by a half-century. the work is the next room in a house combahee built.

## phillis wheatley, attestation page (1773)

**wheatley, phillis.** *poems on various subjects, religious and moral.* london, printed for a. bell, 1773. the attestation page bears the signatures of eighteen white boston men, including john hancock and the governor of massachusetts, certifying that a Black woman had authored the poems.

facsimile available through the library of congress and through documenting the american south at the university of north carolina.

the dissertation reads the attestation page as the structural origin of the rule that Black women's intellectual work must arrive through a verification gate before it can be received as theology. the precedent founded american literature and also founded the suppression of diasporic intelligence as theological category. 256 years later, AI detection tools are the contemporary form of the attestation page. the dissertation operationalizes diasporic intelligence under registers the precedent denied it.

---

## octavia e. butler, *parable of the sower* (1993)

**butler, octavia e.** *parable of the sower.* four walls eight windows, 1993.

butler's lauren oya olamina hand-writes the scripture of a new religion called earthseed while the united states collapses around her. the opening verse — *all that you touch you change. all that you change changes you. the only lasting truth is change. god is change* — is the theological move n. katherine hayles names twenty years later as technogenesis. butler had to write it as fiction because of the wheatley precedent. the dissertation reads earthseed and technogenesis as one operation at two registers.

butler is one of the three Black women i name in the acknowledgements. the other two are toni morrison and toshi reagon.

## toni morrison, *beloved* (1987)

**morrison, toni.** *beloved.* alfred a. knopf, 1987.

morrison shows what cooper-white argues. the ancestors are in the room. the self is plural. the boundary of the individual is porous. the dissertation cites *beloved* as primary source for the claim that Black women's interior life routinely holds the simultaneous co-presence of the living, the dead, and the not-yet-born, and that the clinical, theological, and juridical architectures designed to deny this co-presence misread Black women's truth-telling as pathology.

i return to one paragraph in *beloved* the way the wise auntie returns to one paragraph. the paragraph is sethe in the kitchen telling denver about rememory. that is the diasporic intelligence i name.

## zora neale hurston, *mules and men* (1935)

**hurston, zora neale.** *mules and men.* j. b. lippincott, 1935.

hurston is the methodological ancestor of any contemporary attempt to take Black women's practice as an authored body of knowledge. she returned to eatonville, florida, with a notebook and treated her own people, including conjure practitioners, as authored sources whose practices warranted documentation in their own grammar. she called what she was doing folklore because the wheatley precedent required her to. the dissertation calls what i am doing methodology because the time has changed, and because zora's work is the reason the time has changed.

the personalization layer in the methodology is named ZORAs after her.

## audre lorde, *sister outsider* (1984)

**lorde, audre.** *sister outsider: essays and speeches.* crossing press, 1984.

lorde licenses the use of the practitioner's own life as data. she licenses the silence-into-language-and-action move. she licenses the embodied claim that the personal is theoretically generative when spoken into the room that did not want to hear it. the autoethnographic methodology of the dissertation — the hospital stay, the cognitive split, the eighteen years of stalled drafts — operates inside the architecture lorde built.

*your silence will not protect you* is the line that keeps me writing when i would rather not.

## bell hooks, *all about love* (2000) and *communion* (2002)

**hooks, bell.** *all about love: new visions.* william morrow, 2000.

**hooks, bell.** *communion: the female search for love.* william morrow, 2002.

hooks frames love as an ethical practice and a theological category rather than a feeling. she frames healing as relational restoration, not individual recovery. the dissertation's pastoral counseling argument operates inside her vocabulary. healing is not what one person achieves alone. healing is what a relational field becomes when ancestors, community, and the divine are participants.

## lindsey stewart, *the conjuring of america* (2024)

**stewart, lindsey.** *the conjuring of america: mojos, mermaids, medicine, and 400 years of Black women's magic.* narrated by adenrele ojo, legacy lit, 2025. audible audiobook.

stewart names conjure as Black women's philosophy. the dissertation's lane I (Black women's metaphysical healing traditions) opens with her argument. she refuses the cartesian split between spirit and survival. she treats the practitioner's situated knowledge as ethically and epistemologically authoritative. the digital identity gathering method is a conjure protocol with new tools.

## ruha benjamin, *race after technology* (2019)

**benjamin, ruha.** *race after technology: abolitionist tools for the new jim code.* polity, 2019.

benjamin gives the diagnostic ground the dissertation builds on. the new jim code is the architecture the sisterhood voice data trust is designed against. benjamin's call for abolitionist tools built by and for the populations the dominant tools exclude is the structural charge the work attempts to answer in the consent-governed voice infrastructure register.

---

## linda tuhiwai smith, *decolonizing methodologies* (1999)

**smith, linda tuhiwai.** *decolonizing methodologies: research and indigenous peoples.* zed books, 1999.

smith establishes that research methodology is not neutral. the dominant academic methodologies were designed alongside colonization and reproduce colonization unless they are explicitly redesigned by and with the populations the research concerns. the dissertation's methods chapter — practitioner-research, autoethnography, embedded case study, dialogic AI as method — operates inside her framework.

## the indigora codex (2022)

**cook-kendrick, jasmaine.** *the indigora codex.* 2022. unpublished manuscript.

my own. drafted in 2022 as fiction. the seven prime laws are the cosmology underneath the dissertation, the trust, and the voice bible. the work the academy is reading in 2026 is the operational form of the fiction i wrote in 2022. time is spiral, not linear.

the seven prime laws as a reading appendix:

> I. trauma does not vanish. it reorganizes reality.
> II. time is spiral, not linear. ancestors are cached intelligence.
> III. rest is not leisure. rest is ancestral technology.
> IV. consent is the substrate of every encounter.
> V. déjà vu is not malfunction. it is ancestral recall.
> VI. the greatest defense is shared memory.
> VII. the state of forgetting is the antagonist. the cure is the registry.
